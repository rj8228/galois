## What was measured

- **Floors.** All 8 corners (88,179,840 positions; full table 44 MB, or 3.0 MB reduced by 16 symmetries, with identical values). 6 edges with orientation (42,577,920; 21.3 MB) and 7 edges (510,935,040; 255 MB), each read through several symmetries. Kociemba's coordinates: corner twist, edge flip, UD-slice, their three pairs, and phase 1 (twist × flip × slice: 2.2 billion raw entries, stored as 140,908,410 after 16-fold symmetry reduction; 70 MB). Every table can also be stored at 2 bits per entry (distance mod 3), half the size.
- **Correctness.** Every floor checked against the true distance at all 8,240,087 positions within 6 moves. The search checked to return exactly the true distance on those positions. Every benchmark solution replayed by the solver and by cubing.js. Superflip must come out at exactly 20.
- **Benchmark.** IDA* on 50 scrambles each of 12, 14 and 16 random moves, 20 random-state scrambles from cubing.js, and superflip. For each floor set: memory, mean and maximum nodes, time per solve, solution lengths.

*Nodes* are positions generated by the search, each one looked up in the floors (or, one move from the end, compared with solved). Each solve runs on one thread with four solves running at once, except superflip, which runs on all four threads. Times are native C; expect a WebAssembly build in the browser to be slower per node.
